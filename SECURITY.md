# Security & Ethical Disclosure Policy

## 🛡️ Responsible Disclosure

The **Resplicon** project adheres strictly to ethical hacking, defensive security principles, and international vulnerability disclosure standards.

### Supported Scenarios
- **Educational Environments**: Universities, cybersecurity labs, CTF events, and offensive/defensive training courses.
- **Authorized Audits**: Verification of your own domain, infrastructure, or assets under your administrative control.
- **Defensive OPSEC**: Auditing personal digital footprints and public records exposure.

### Reporting Vulnerabilities
If you discover a security vulnerability or exploit risk within the Resplicon engine:
1. Please **do not** open a public issue.
2. Submit a responsible disclosure report directly to the repository maintainers.
3. Maintainers will verify the issue, patch the vulnerability, and release an update.

## ⚖️ Legal & Ethical Compliance
Resplicon is designed for defensive intelligence and educational awareness. Users are solely responsible for ensuring compliance with applicable regional and international cybercrime legislation before initiating any reconnaissance scans.
